blue scheme. Walls in deep midnight navy stretched fabric (#242e3d), cabinetry
and joinery in near-black ink navy shaker style (#1a2130) with aged brass
hardware and trim (#a8894f), warm oak shelf accents (#8a6743), charcoal deep-
